FahrKlar is an app for practising the German driving theory test (class B). It is not affiliated with TÜV, DEKRA or any authority.

2. No account, data stays on your device

FahrKlar needs no account. Learning progress, exam results, exam date, language and reminder settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

The app uses no analytics or tracking tool operated by us.

3. Reminders

If you turn on study reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No data is sent to us or third parties. You can turn reminders off in the app or in system settings at any time. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. This may involve processing your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and interactions with ads.

  • On first launch, Google's consent dialog (User Messaging Platform) asks whether personalised ads are allowed. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • On iOS, the app also asks via Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, the advertising ID is not used.
  • You can change your choice in the app settings and in the privacy settings of Android or iOS.

Legal basis: consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG; for non-personalised ads, our legitimate interest in funding the free version (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google may transfer data to the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from apps.

FahrKlar Pro loads no ads.

5. Buying FahrKlar Pro

The one-time purchase runs through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment details are processed only by Google or Apple; we never see them.

To verify the purchase and restore it on new devices, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). It processes a random, anonymous user ID, store receipts, device and app information, and the IP address. We do not receive your name or email this way. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; receipts are kept for statutory retention periods (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: revenuecat.com/privacy.

6. App stores

When you download the app, Google (privacy) and Apple (privacy) process data under their own responsibility, such as your store account. We have no influence on this.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Write to info@premiumyazilim.com.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority in the EU, in particular the one where you live.

8. Children

FahrKlar is meant for people preparing for the driving test (from 16, accompanied driving from 17). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
